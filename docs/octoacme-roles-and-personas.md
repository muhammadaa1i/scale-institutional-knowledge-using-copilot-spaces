# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Test Engineers

### Role Summary
QA / Test Engineers own test strategy and verify that delivered work meets acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans and automate regression coverage
- Validate acceptance criteria before release
- Triage and report defects with clear reproduction steps

### Goals
- Catch defects early and keep escaped defects low
- Give the team a reliable quality signal

### Typical Communication
- Defect reports and test results in sprint reviews
- Acceptance sign-off in pull requests

### Interactions with other roles
- **Developers**: agree on testability, fix and re-verify defects
- **Product Managers**: clarify acceptance criteria and sign-off
- **Release / DevOps Engineers**: provide go/no-go quality input for releases

---

## Designers / UX Researchers

### Role Summary
Designers / UX Researchers shape how the product looks and behaves, grounded in research with real users.

### Responsibilities
- Run user research and usability testing
- Produce wireframes, prototypes and design specs
- Review implemented work against the intended experience

### Goals
- Make the product usable and consistent
- Reduce rework by validating ideas before build

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and research readouts
- Annotated prototypes attached to backlog items

### Interactions with other roles
- **Product Managers**: frame problems and validate solutions together
- **Developers**: hand off specs and review the built result

---

## Release / DevOps Engineers

### Role Summary
Release / DevOps Engineers own build, deployment and operational readiness so changes reach production safely.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain CI/CD pipelines and environments
- Run the deployment checklist and rollback plan
- Lead incident response and post-incident follow-up

### Goals
- Frequent, low-risk releases
- Fast recovery when something goes wrong

### Typical Communication
- Release notes and deployment announcements
- Incident updates and post-incident reviews

### Interactions with other roles
- **Developers**: support pipelines and release readiness
- **Project Managers**: align release dates and communicate status
- **QA / Test Engineers**: confirm quality gates before deploy

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads / Architects set technical direction and guard the long-term health of the codebase.

### Responsibilities
- Lead design reviews and record key technical decisions
- Identify technical risks and dependencies early
- Mentor Developers on standards and patterns

### Goals
- Sound, maintainable architecture
- Technical trade-offs that are visible and agreed

### Typical Communication
- Design docs and decision records
- Technical risk updates in planning

### Interactions with other roles
- **Developers**: review designs and guide implementation
- **Product Managers**: explain cost and constraints of trade-offs
- **Project Managers**: feed technical risks into the risk register

---

## Stakeholders / Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary
Stakeholders / Executive Sponsors provide funding, priorities and final decisions on escalations.

### Responsibilities
- Approve scope, budget and major changes
- Decide on escalated risks and trade-offs
- Remove organizational blockers

### Goals
- Projects that deliver the intended business outcomes
- Timely, informed decisions

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and ad-hoc escalations
- Steering reviews at key milestones

### Interactions with other roles
- **Project Managers**: receive status reports and decide on escalations
- **Product Managers**: confirm priorities and success metrics

---
